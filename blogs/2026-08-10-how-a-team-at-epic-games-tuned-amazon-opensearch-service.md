Learn how a team at Epic Games tuned their Amazon OpenSearch Service cluster for Fortnite analytics. This post details how Epic Games partnered with AWS to right-size instances, rebalance shards, optimize index mappings, and upgrade engine and Graviton versions, improving query latency and throughput while reducing costs.
