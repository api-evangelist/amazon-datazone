The KIP-848 consumer protocol in Apache Kafka 4.0 redesigns consumer group rebalancing to eliminate stop-the-world pauses. This post explains how the consumer protocol works on Amazon MSK, how to enable it on MSK Standard and Express brokers, and how to diagnose and resolve slow rebalancing issues.
