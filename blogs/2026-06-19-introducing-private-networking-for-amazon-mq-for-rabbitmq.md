The Private Networking feature for Amazon MQ for RabbitMQ lets brokers establish outbound connections to private resources in your VPC without exposing those resources publicly. The post walks through connecting to private identity providers, federating messages between brokers, and linking to self-hosted RabbitMQ instances using VPC Lattice, AWS RAM, and PrivateLink.
