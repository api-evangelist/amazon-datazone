AWS is announcing the AWS Data Analytics plugin for the new Data agent in ChatGPT Work. Teams can ask questions in natural language, analyze governed data across their Amazon Redshift data warehouse and data lakes, and build shareable dashboards, all from a conversation in ChatGPT Work.
