This guide covers migrating Amazon Redshift RA3 clusters to Graviton-based RG instances, which deliver up to 2.2x faster performance for data warehouse workloads and cost 30% less per vCPU. It compares three migration strategies—Elastic Resize, Classic Resize, and Snapshot/Restore—with node mapping recommendations and best practices.
