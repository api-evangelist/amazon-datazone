On May 12, 2026, we announced the general availability of Amazon Redshift RG instances, powered by AWS Graviton processors. RG instances are up to 2.2x as fast for data warehouse workloads and up to 2.4x as fast for data lake workloads, all at 30% lower price per vCPU compared to RA3 instances. RG instances support all data lake formats supported by RA3 and eliminate Amazon Redshift Spectrum’s per-TB scanning charges.
